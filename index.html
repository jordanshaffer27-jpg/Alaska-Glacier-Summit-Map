Alaska Glacier & Summit Explorer

Explore geographic relationships between named Glaciers and Mountain Summits across Alaska.

About the Datasets

Glaciers: Named Glacier locations from the U.S. Geographic Names dataset. Their coordinates show the distribution of named Glaciers across Alaska. Elevation is included when available.

Summits: Named Mountain Summit locations from the same geographic names dataset. Their locations and elevations help show where mountains occur near Glaciers.

Both datasets represent named geographic features rather than complete inventories of every Glacier or Mountain Summit in Alaska.

Method

The map calculates the distance from each Glacier to its nearest Mountain Summit using the Haversine formula. It counts glaciers within 10, 25, and 50 kilometers of a summit and calculates the median distance. Click a glacier marker to see its nearest summit and the distance between them. Turn on the connection layer to display the connecting line.

Interpretation

This analysis compares the locations of named Glaciers and Mountain Summits in Alaska. The results show geographic proximity, not actual Glacier boundaries or changes in Glacier size. Results are limited to the geographic features included in the datasets.

Data: U.S. Board on Geographic Names / USGS GNIS.

Map tiles: OpenStreetMap, Esri, and OpenTopoMap.
